Delete your Morgap account

Morgap users can request deletion of their account and associated data directly in the app.

  1. Open Morgap and sign in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Read the notice, select the confirmation checkbox, and tap Yes.

Confirming the request deletes the account and associated personal information. Some order or transaction records may be retained temporarily when required by law, financial obligations, or fraud-prevention requirements, then deleted or anonymized when the retention period ends.

If you cannot access the app, request assistance on WhatsApp at +965 6584 1184. We may request limited information to verify account ownership before completing the request.
